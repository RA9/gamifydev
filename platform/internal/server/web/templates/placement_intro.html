Before you choose a path

The placement diagnostic

This tells us where to start you — not whether you're good enough. There's no pass mark to be afraid of: answer honestly and you'll land in the right place.

What to expect

  • {{ index .Data "minutes" }} minutes, timed from when you begin. The clock runs on our server, so closing the tab won't pause it.
  • Multiple choice, across seven topics — from programming basics to how computers work.
  • Questions are drawn at random, so no two sittings are quite the same.
  • Skip anything you don't know. A guess costs you nothing, but an honest blank places you better.

What happens with the result

  • It picks your starting path and may let you skip courses you've clearly already got.
  • If the fundamentals aren't there yet, you'll start on Computer Science Foundations — that's the point of the test, not a punishment.
  • It never unlocks a course on its own. You still pass each course's checkpoint the normal way.

Because of that last rule, there's nothing to gain by looking answers up — you'd only be routed past material you'd then have to pass anyway.
