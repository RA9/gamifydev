Our story

We're building the school we wish we had.

GamifyDev started from a simple frustration: learning to code shouldn't feel like reading a textbook alone in a room. It should feel like building something real, with people who care if you succeed.

{{ icon "layers" }}

The journey

We started as developers who struggled through the same broken cycle: watch a video, copy some code, feel lost the moment something breaks. Tutorials taught syntax but never taught thinking.

So we built GamifyDev — a platform where every lesson is a building block toward a real project. Where you write code that gets reviewed by a human, not just auto-graded by a bot. Where competitions push you to solve problems under pressure, the way real engineering does.

{{ icon "compass" }}

Our mission

Make high-quality, hands-on coding education accessible to everyone — regardless of background, budget, or location. We believe the best way to learn is to build, get feedback, and iterate.

Every course on GamifyDev is structured around projects, not just concepts. You don't just learn what a function is — you write one that solves a real problem, submit it, and hear back from a mentor who's shipped production code.

{{ icon "sparkles" }}

What makes us different

  • Mentor-graded code — Real humans review your work and give you feedback that moves you forward.
  • Live competitions — Timed challenges with leaderboards that test your skills against other builders.
  • Rich, multimedia lessons — Video, audio, and interactive content — not walls of text.
  • Community-first — A forum where you ask questions, share your work, and help others grow.
  • Certificates that mean something — Earn them by shipping projects, not watching videos.
{{ icon "flag" }}

Where we're going

This is just the beginning. We're building toward a future where GamifyDev is the place you go from "I've never written a line of code" to "I just got my first dev job" — and then keep coming back to sharpen your skills.

Here's what's on our roadmap:

  • Team challenges — Collaborate with other learners on group projects.
  • AI-assisted hints — Get unstuck faster without spoiling the learning.
  • Industry tracks — Specialized paths for web, mobile, data, and systems engineering.
  • Job board — Connect our top builders directly with companies that hire for skill, not credentials.
  • Open curriculum — Let the community contribute lessons, assignments, and challenges.
